Three fields, one grid

Each of these is backed by peer-reviewed publication, operational responsibility at PLN, or both — not by interest alone.

Energy transition

Research and policy analysis on how Indonesia moves to a cleaner, cheaper, more reliable electricity system — and what the distribution network has to become for that to work.

  • Distributed solar PV: optimal siting and capacity on radial feeders, and the investment case behind it (Energy Procedia, Elsevier)
  • Net-metering policy for medium-voltage industrial prosumers (IEEE CGEE 2022, Istanbul)
  • Dynamic and Time-of-Use tariff design for industry (AIP Conference Proceedings)
  • Demand-side management and renewable integration
  • Rooftop-solar adoption and socio-economic factors in developing-country cities (Journal of Electrical Systems)
  • Waste-to-energy — Australia Awards short course, Griffith University

Electric vehicles (EV)

His current doctoral research at Monash University — Pathways to Advancing Sustainable Transportation: A Focus on Indonesia's EV Ecosystem — is carried out under the RACE for EVs program and supervised at Monash Art, Design and Architecture.

  • What charging infrastructure Indonesia actually needs, and where
  • Electricity tariffs and their effect on EV adoption and charging behaviour
  • Urban form and transport planning as constraints on EV scale-up
  • Grid-side impact of EV load on distribution networks

AI & data analytics for energy

Machine learning built and deployed inside a utility — models that change what field crews do the next morning, not dashboards that get looked at once.

  • Electricity-theft detection: a hybrid stacking-ensemble model, field-validated in West Java
  • CERDAS — complaint prediction from customer-report keywords
  • Medium-term load forecasting: neural networks vs. splitted linear regression in tropical grids (IEEE)
  • Smart metering and AMI/AMR analytics; a tamper-aware STM32 meter with GPS/GPRS telemetry
  • GIS and VIIRS satellite night-lights asset mapping — Esri Fellowship 2026
  • EnVisor — an AI energy-efficiency advisor for households and industry

Findings you can quote

Specific, sourced results from the work — useful for journalists, researchers and policy teams.

A machine-learning ranking model for electricity-theft detection lifted PLN field-inspection hit rates from roughly 15% to roughly 77%, recovering lost revenue on a network without smart meters.

PT PLN (Persero) · PLN National Innovation Awards 2024, 3rd place

Indonesia's static 1:0.65 net-metering export ratio penalises industrial prosumers: a 21 MW Bekasi case showed a cumulative consumer loss of −USD 2,102 over two years. The conclusion is that Indonesia needs dynamic net metering rather than a fixed punitive multiplier.

IEEE CGEE 2022, Istanbul · Bekasi 2020–2022

Time-of-Use tariffs across the Java–Madura–Bali interconnection could cut daily generation cost by ~2.59% — roughly Rp 3.6 trillion per year. Thirty industrial customers in PLN Bekasi showed mid-peak demand elasticity of −0.3, meaning they do shift load when the incentive is real.

Dynamic tariff scenario analysis · Universitas Indonesia

Indonesia holds around 40% of the world's geothermal potential but uses under 10% of it — the gap that the EnerBit work explores as an energy-monetization problem.
